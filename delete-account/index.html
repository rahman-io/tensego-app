Delete Your TenseGO Account

This page explains how to delete your account and data for the TenseGO: Smart English Tutor app, developed by REPLACE.ID.


Option 1: Delete your account in the app (recommended, instant)

If you can still open the TenseGO app on your phone, follow these steps:

  1. Open the TenseGO app and log in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to the Profil tab.
  3. Tap the account options menu, then select Hapus Akun (Delete Account).
  4. Enter your account password to confirm.
  5. Tap the confirm button. Your account and data will be permanently deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you are unable to access the app (for example, you uninstalled it or lost access to your device), you can request deletion by email:

  1. Send an email to cyberefforts@gmail.com from the same email address associated with your TenseGO account.
  2. Use the subject line "TenseGO Account Deletion Request" and include the email address of the account you want deleted.
  3. We will process your request and confirm once it is completed, normally within 30 days.

What data is deleted
  • Your account credentials (email and authentication record)
  • Your learning profile (name, learning goal, CEFR level, chosen theme/style, avatar)
  • Your learning progress, quiz history, and AI quota/usage records
  • Your locally stored chat/history data on the device where the app is installed

What data may be retained, and for how long
  • Purchase and subscription records are kept by Google Play Billing according to Google's own retention policy; these are not stored on our servers and cannot be deleted by us directly. See Google Play Billing for details.
  • Aggregated, anonymized usage statistics that can no longer be linked back to you may be retained for product improvement purposes.
  • Backups that already contain your data are rotated out and fully removed within 30 days of your deletion request.

For any question about this process, contact the developer at cyberefforts@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy for more information on how your data is handled.
